# Agro Nova: Regenerative Agricultural Intelligence

**Agro Nova** is a regenerative-agriculture assistant for smallholder farmers in India. It is built to extend to Brazil, Russia and China, and the same Flutter code runs on Android, iOS and the web.

1. **Plot capture:** a farmer marks four field corners by tapping the map, using GPS, typing coordinates or searching a place. The backend validates the polygon (shape, area, country) with Shapely and pyproj.
2. **Soil:** it shows public soil data from ISRIC SoilGrids, lets the farmer enter government or lab sample values with a GPS point, and explains how to get soil tested locally.
3. **Weather and climate:** a crop-specific outlook combines a 10-day forecast with satellite NDVI, rainfall against normal and soil moisture from Google Earth Engine (CHIRPS, ERA5-Land, SMAP). It falls back to Open-Meteo, and includes El Niño / La Niña effects per country.
4. **Plant diagnosis:** a leaf photo goes to an AI vision model (OpenAI, then Anthropic, then Gemini, whichever is configured and answers first), which identifies the likely deficiency, pest or disease. The app returns an organic remedy with preparation steps and explains why organic beats chemical.
5. **What to grow next:** an AI model recommends crops for the next local sowing season (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid and so on), grounded in the plot's soil, satellite data, ENSO state and location.
6. **Resilience, water saving and value addition:** each has a generic guide (compost, biogas, milk, water tips, market ideas) plus an AI section personalised to the plot, crop and livestock. The app also has a cow calculator.
7. **Schemes and alerts:** a government schemes and subsidies catalogue, with scheme, weather and market push notifications sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Slow AI answers run as background jobs, and a farmer who leaves the app gets a "ready" notification that opens the finished answer.
8. **Languages:** 22 Indian languages plus Portuguese, Russian and Chinese. Screen labels are built into the app; for the widely used languages the AI writes advice directly in the farmer's language, and other dynamic text is translated by the Cloud Translation API and cached.
9. **Backend stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, Pydantic and SQLAlchemy (SQLite locally, Postgres for production, Alembic migrations). AI calls go to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini in a configurable order, each with retries or a circuit breaker; it also uses `earthengine-api` and `firebase-admin`. Optional Redis shares caches and rate limits between instances, Cloud Tasks runs jobs on any instance, and a nightly job precomputes satellite data per plot. Prometheus metrics, alert rules and a local Grafana dashboard are included. It is tested with pytest (SQLite, Postgres and Redis) and designed for Cloud Run.
10. **Privacy and offline use:** the app shows a versioned privacy notice and records a separate choice for plots and farm details, AI advice and notifications (India's DPDP Act), and offers data download and erasure; inactive accounts are erased after a set period. With no signal it shows the answers it saved and queues plots and soil samples to send later, without duplicates.
11. **App stack, auth and data:** Flutter with Provider, `google_maps_flutter`, geolocator, image_picker and Firebase Auth, Messaging and App Check. The backend supports a no-credentials dev login or Firebase login, and every Google service is optional and fails soft. An Earth Engine to BigQuery pipeline for climatology (future Vertex AI seasonal model) is included.
